Stickwork Drills — Visual Guide

Top-down view of every carpark drill. Amber solid line = the ball. Green dashed line = you. The watch beeps each 30-second block; these pictures are what to do inside it.
Setup, every session: futsal shoes · stick + ball · one cone placed ~5 steps to the LEFT of your wall spot · stand ~3 steps from the wall unless the drill says otherwise.
MODE A — fresh skill quality: do these FIRST, HR under 150, slow the feet if it nags.  MODE B — tired skill under fatigue: done straight AFTER hard running, uncapped — being out of breath is the point.

1 · Wall push + first touch MODE A

4 × 30s · the push is the serve — the TRAP is the drill
  • Soft hands — give with the ball so it stops dead, no bounce-off.
  • Look up before each receive, not after.

2 · Receive → switch to the cone MODE A

4 × 30s · the G2/G3 fix — the left-to-right transfer
  • LEAD the target — sweep to where the runner is going, not where he is.
  • Call the target out loud before you receive.

3 · Indian dribble MODE A

3 × 30s · moving forward at a controlled jog
  • Ball crosses your body left-right-left as you jog forward.
  • Change pace mid-rep; no sharp cuts (knee).

4 · V-pulls MODE A

3 × 30s · stationary — the escape-the-presser move
  • Soft hands, low hips — the ball never leaves the stick.
  • This is the move that takes you past a lunging presser.

5 · Stop-start carry MODE A

3 × 30s · controlled, NOT sprints
  • Carry close, stop the ball dead, restart smoothly — no sprint out.

6 · Jab-tackle rhythm MODE B on hard days

2 × 30s · your best weapon, kept greased
  • Low stance, stick flicks out and returns — like a snake strike.

7 · Tackle-transition MODE B — NEW (the Josh fix)

Added 17 Aug · win it LOW, then RISE and PASS in one flow
  • Low hips, LIVE feet — feet under you, not lunged. Test: could you stand up in one beat?
  • The tackle isn't over at the steal — it's over when the pass leaves.
